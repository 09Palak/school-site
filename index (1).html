About Us

A school built around how children actually grow.

For over two decades, Meridian Hill has combined a rigorous academic foundation with a genuine focus on curiosity, character and confidence. Our 12-acre campus brings together modern classrooms, expansive playing fields and dedicated spaces for art, music and science — all shaped around the developmental needs of each age group.

We believe a great education is not measured only in results, but in the kind of adults our students become.

Our Vision

To nurture confident, compassionate learners equipped to shape a changing world.

Our Mission

To deliver an education that balances academic depth with creativity, values and wellbeing.

About Meridian Hill School

Founded in 1998, Meridian Hill School has grown from a single block of classrooms into a 12-acre campus serving over 2,000 students from Pre-Primary through Grade 12. We are affiliated with the CBSE board and offer the IB Diploma Programme in our senior years.

Our Vision: To nurture confident, compassionate learners equipped to shape a changing world through knowledge, empathy and courage.

Our Mission: To deliver an education that balances academic depth with creativity, strong values and genuine wellbeing — supported by outstanding faculty and modern facilities.

Our Approach: Small class sizes, individual mentorship, and a belief that every child learns differently. We combine structured academics with the arts, sport and community service so students leave not just qualified, but ready.

Principal's Full Message

Dear Parents and Students,

Every child who walks through our gates carries a different spark — a curiosity, a talent, a way of seeing the world. Our work at Meridian Hill is simply to protect that spark, and give it room to grow.

We do not believe in a single definition of success. Some of our students will excel in mathematics, others on the football field, others in a paintbrush or a debate podium. Our role is to give each of them the guidance, structure and encouragement to become the best version of themselves.

I invite you to visit our campus, meet our faculty, and see for yourself the warmth and rigour that define a Meridian Hill education.

Dr. Anjali Rao Mehta
Principal, Meridian Hill School
